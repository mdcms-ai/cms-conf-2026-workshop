Welcome to Field Notes. This is where we write down what we learn while
building our product.

## Why a blog

Writing forces us to explain decisions. If we cannot explain one, it was
probably not a good decision.

## What comes next

Every post here is a Markdown file in the repository. That works well for the
three engineers who write them. It works less well for everyone else.
